# Hören — Structured German Listening

A minimal, chapter-gated German listening practice studio structured around **Netzwerk neu A1 & A2 (Kapitel 1–12)**.

## Features
- **Zero Out-of-Syllabus Voiceovers:** 30 multi-speaker D-A-CH neural conversations for **A1 Kapitel 1** covering **100% (203 / 203)** of the chapter's vocabulary across 5 thematic categories.
- **Interactive Synced Subtitles:** Live word-by-word highlighting; click any spoken word to pause and inspect its article, plural, part of speech, English meaning, and chapter origin.
- **Hidden-by-Default Clip Vocabulary Drawer:** Distraction-free listening by default; open `Words` anytime to view the current clip's vocabulary grouped by Noun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb, Preposition, and Phrase.
- **Chapter Prep Hub:** Complete **Netzwerk A1 (1,962 words)** and **A2 (1,486 words)** chapter-by-chapter vocabulary and grammar reference.
- **Combine Chapters (Mixer):** Select multiple chapters (`Kap. 1 + 2`, `Kap. 1 + 3`, `Kap. 1 + 2 + 3`) to unlock cross-chapter conversations restricted strictly to your chosen syllabus.

